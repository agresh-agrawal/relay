The handoff

A short brief, not a chat dump

The next AI gets what a fresh pair of hands needs and nothing else, so it goes straight to work.

  • About 640 tokens for a three-hour session
  • Your rules, word for word, like “keep the nav sticky”
  • Keys and passwords hidden before anything leaves your PC
  • You choose the first move: finish, fix problems, test or review

How it works

Three steps, and two of them happen on their own

  1. 1

    Install it. It finds your chats.

    A small Relay button appears on the edge of your screen. Relay reads the chats Claude Code, Codex and Antigravity already save, and never changes them.

  2. 2

    Work as usual. Relay keeps watch.

    The button’s dot turns red when an AI stops. Click it to see your 5-hour limits and how full each chat’s memory is. Relay saves a handoff in your project every few minutes, even while it’s closed.

  3. 3

    When an AI stops, pick the next one.

    Press Ctrl Alt R or tap Continue in another AI. Relay opens your project there and copies a starting message. Paste, and it carries on.

Features

Small panel, a lot less starting over

Your 5-hour limits

See when Claude’s 5-hour window resets and roughly how much is used. Codex shows its exact numbers, weekly too.

Handoff always saved

While an AI works, Relay keeps .relay/HANDOFF.md up to date in the project. A crash or a sudden limit never loses your place.

Secrets stay home

API keys, tokens and passwords are found and hidden before a handoff leaves your PC. Your own AI keys live in Windows Credential Manager.

No API key needed

Built-in rules write the handoff offline. Add a key for Anthropic, OpenAI, Gemini or OpenRouter, or use Ollama, for an even sharper brief.

Reminds you at reset

When a limit resets, the edge tab turns green and Relay offers to bring the newest work back to that AI.

Pick the next move

One tap tells the next AI where to start: finish what’s left, fix problems first, test everything, or review before changing.

Screenshots too

Press Ctrl Alt S to add what’s on screen. Images from the chat come along, so the next AI sees what you saw.

One click away

The button on the screen edge opens Relay over your work. Drag it up or down; it hides for full-screen videos and games. Closed, Relay uses about 50 MB.

Privacy

Everything stays on your PC

Relay has no account, no sign-up and no tracking. It only talks to the internet if you add your own AI key, and then only to the provider you chose.

  • Read-only. Relay reads the chat files your AI apps already save. It never edits them.
  • Secrets removed. Keys, tokens and passwords are taken out of every handoff before it leaves.
  • Keys locked away. Your AI keys go to Windows Credential Manager, never into files.
  • Password managers skipped. The optional activity timeline ignores them, and you can pause it any time.

FAQ

Questions

Do I need an API key?

No. Relay writes the handoff with its own rules, offline. If you add a key (Anthropic, OpenAI, Gemini, OpenRouter, Ollama or any OpenAI-compatible server), it can write a clearer brief.

How does it know my 5-hour limit?

For Claude, Relay counts usage in your Claude Code chats to see when the current 5-hour window started and when it resets. After you’ve hit the limit once, it estimates how much is used. Codex reports exact percentages, and Relay shows them as they are. Chats on claude.ai share the limit but aren’t visible to Relay.

Will it get in the way of my windows?

No. Relay is a small button on the edge of your screen. Click it and the panel opens over your windows; click it again or press Esc and it’s gone. Drag the button up or down if it covers something. It steps aside for full-screen videos and games.

What happens to my code and chats?

They stay on your PC. Relay writes the handoff into a .relay folder inside your project, and that folder ignores itself in git. Older handoffs are kept in .relay/history.

Does it work with ChatGPT, Claude.ai or Gemini in the browser?

Yes, as a destination: Relay copies the whole handoff as one message and opens the site. As a source, copy the web conversation and choose “Paste chat” in Relay. A browser extension is planned.

Is there a Mac version?

Not yet. Relay is Windows only for now.

How do I quit or uninstall it?

Quit from the tray icon (Quit Relay). Uninstall from Windows Settings → Apps. Your settings stay in %APPDATA%\com.relay.app until you delete that folder.
