The handoff

A short brief, not a chat dump.

The next AI gets exactly what a fresh pair of hands needs, and goes straight to work.

  • About 640 tokens for a three-hour session
  • Your rules, word for word, like “keep the nav sticky”
  • Keys and passwords removed before anything leaves your PC
  • You choose the first move: finish, fix, test or review

Features

Built for the moment your AI stops.

01

Knows your limits

See when Claude’s 5-hour window resets and roughly how much is used, Codex’s exact numbers, and how full each chat’s memory is. When a limit resets, Relay offers to bring the newest work back.

02

Picks up exactly where it stopped

Relay reads the chat your AI already saved and keeps what matters: your goal, your rules, what’s done, what’s left, where it stopped and which files changed. Tap any part to read or edit it before it goes.

03

One click to the next AI

Pick Antigravity, VS Code, Cursor, a terminal agent or a chat website. Relay saves the handoff in your project, opens the app and copies a short starting message. Paste, and it carries on.

04

Always one click away

A small button lives on the edge of your screen. Click it and Relay opens over your work; click again and it’s gone. Its dot turns red when an AI stops. Drag it anywhere along the edge, and it hides for full-screen videos and games.

FAQ

Questions, answered.

Something else? Ask on GitHub.

Do I need an API key?

No. Relay writes the handoff with its own rules, offline. If you add a key (Anthropic, OpenAI, Gemini, OpenRouter, Ollama or any OpenAI-compatible server), it can write a clearer brief.

How does it know my 5-hour limit?

For Claude, Relay counts usage in your Claude Code chats to see when the current 5-hour window started and when it resets. After you’ve hit the limit once, it estimates how much is used. Codex reports exact percentages, and Relay shows them as they are. Chats on claude.ai share the limit but aren’t visible to Relay.

Will it get in the way of my windows?

No. Relay is a small button on the edge of your screen. Click it and the panel opens over your windows; click it again or press Esc and it’s gone. Drag the button up or down if it covers something. It steps aside for full-screen videos and games.

What happens to my code and chats?

They stay on your PC. Relay writes the handoff into a .relay folder inside your project, and that folder ignores itself in git. Older handoffs are kept in .relay/history.

Does it work with ChatGPT, Claude.ai or Gemini in the browser?

Yes, as a destination: Relay copies the whole handoff as one message and opens the site. As a source, copy the web conversation and choose “Bring in a web chat” in Relay. A browser extension is planned.

Windows blocked it completely. What now?

Some Windows 11 PCs have Smart App Control turned on, which blocks every app that isn’t code-signed, with no “Run anyway” button. Relay will never ask you to change security settings. A signed release is on the way.

Is there a Mac version?

Not yet. Relay is Windows only for now.

How do I quit or uninstall it?

Quit from the tray icon (Quit Relay). Uninstall from Windows Settings → Apps. Your settings stay in %APPDATA%\com.relay.app until you delete that folder.
